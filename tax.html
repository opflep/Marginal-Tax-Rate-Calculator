What changed for 2026: the lowest federal rate drops to 14% for the full year (14.5% blended in 2025), and BC raises its lowest rate from 5.06% to 5.60%. Both federal and BC brackets are indexed (federal 2.0%, BC 2.2%). BC credits are also valued at 5.60%, and the BC tax reduction rises to $690.

  • Marginal rate is the tax on your next $100 of income, so it includes the federal BPA phase-out and the BC tax reduction clawback. Statutory bracket rate is shown underneath.
  • Credits modelled: basic personal amounts, Canada employment amount, CPP (base) and EI credits, BC tax reduction. Enhanced CPP and CPP2 are deducted from income.
  • Not modelled: dividends, capital gains, pension/age/disability credits, the federal top-up credit, OAS clawback and benefits such as the Canada workers benefit.
  • Sources: CRA tax brackets, BC tax rates, BC basic credits.
